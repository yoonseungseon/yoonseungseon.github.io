Working Papers

The Impact of Export Demand Shocks on Domestic Used Car Markets: Evidence from South Korea
with Jay Pil Choi, Seung-Hyun Hong, and Seung Hoon Lee
Abstract[PDF]

After the Russia–Ukraine war, Korean used-car exports to Russia surged as used cars became substitutes for new cars, whose supply in Russia had collapsed. Using vehicle-registration panel data and trade statistics, we study how this export demand shock reallocated vehicles across domestic dealer and private-to-private channels, and we develop a model with dealer capacity constraints to explain the price and volume patterns.

Air Pollution and Health Burdens across Borders: Evidence from South Korea after China's Plastic Waste Import Ban
with Jaerim Choi
Abstract[PDF]

China's 2018 plastic waste import ban redirected waste flows toward South Korea. Using a continuous difference-in-differences design with a shift-share instrument, we find that a one-standard-deviation increase in regional import exposure raised local PM2.5 by 2.0–2.6% and monthly health claims by 1.6%.
